## WEQ BPS minutes, 17 October 2025 (draft), paragraph 18 (file weq_bps_2025-10-17_draft.docx)

> [...] as under the WLR Process, curtailments and relief obligations are assigned to flowing transactions that have a positive impact of 5% or greater on a constraint, which a counterflow would not.

Used by: workshop4_loading_relief.ipynb. Supports: A participant, as the minutes report it, suggested that the standards need not address counterflows, because under the WLR Process only flowing transactions with a positive impact are assigned relief obligations. The minutes record the suggestion and do not state a rule.
