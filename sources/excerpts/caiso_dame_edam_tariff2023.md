## Section 33.11.1 (PDF p. 434)

> Congestion revenue will be collected when a Transmission Constraint or intertie scheduling limit binds at different locations of the transmission system and the LMP varies across a Balancing Authority Area in the EDAM Area.

Used by: no notebook; kept for the explanation of the collection of congestion revenue in EDAM. Supports: Congestion revenue arises when either a transmission constraint or an intertie scheduling limit binds.

Used by: no notebook; kept for the explanation of the constraints that make up each area's contribution. Supports: The contribution of each area follows the location of the constraints it manages.
